HOME PAGE BACKGROUND VIDEO
==========================
Put short video files in THIS folder (client/public) with these exact names:

   hero-family.mp4     a happy family eating / enjoying food together
   hero-students.mp4   friends or students eating, laughing, hanging out

They play one after the other, in a loop. If a file is missing it is skipped.
If none exist, an animated gradient with floating momos is shown instead.

Tips
- Free videos: pexels.com/videos or pixabay.com/videos (search "family dinner", "students friends eating").
  Check the licence on the download page.
- Keep each clip 10 to 20 seconds, MP4 (H.264), 1280x720 or 1920x1080, under about 6 MB so phones load fast.
- No sound is needed. The video is always muted.
